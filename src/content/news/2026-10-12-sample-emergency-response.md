## What makes a good field story

- Open with what happened, where and when — and who was reached.
- Add one concrete human detail (with informed consent) or a quote from a community member or team leader.
- Close with what comes next and a link to the relevant program page.

Add real photos to `public/images/` and update the `image` field in this file's frontmatter. Create a new `.md`
file in `src/content/news/` for every new story — the homepage will always show the three most recent posts
automatically.
