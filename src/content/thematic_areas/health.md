LOCA works to expand equitable access to the essential services every family depends on — safe water, primary
health care, nutrition support and education — with particular attention to remote, displaced and underserved
communities.

## What this program delivers

- Safe water supply, water quality, sanitation, hygiene promotion and community-led WASH management
- Primary health care outreach and community health promotion, in coordination with authorised health providers and public systems
- Maternal, newborn and child health linkages, referral support and community-level health awareness
- Nutrition screening and referral, infant and young child feeding promotion, and prevention of malnutrition in coordination with qualified partners
- Education in emergencies, access to inclusive and safe learning, school rehabilitation and community engagement
- Teacher support, foundational learning, youth learning pathways and referral to technical and vocational education and training (TVET)
