LOCA's humanitarian and protection programming places affected communities at the center of assessment,
planning, implementation and accountability. We prioritize people facing the greatest barriers to safety,
services and opportunity — including displaced families, women and children, and persons with disabilities.

## What this program delivers

- Emergency preparedness, early warning, rapid needs assessments and emergency response
- Life-saving relief, including emergency cash assistance, essential household support and referral services
- Protection mainstreaming, safeguarding, child protection and prevention of gender-based violence through safe, survivor-centered referral pathways
- Community-based protection, social cohesion, conflict sensitivity and access to civil documentation and services
- Accountability to affected populations (AAP), community feedback and response mechanisms, and participation in local decision-making
- Inclusion of persons with disabilities and other groups facing heightened barriers
