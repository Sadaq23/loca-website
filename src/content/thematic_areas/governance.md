LOCA invests in the systems and capacities that make change sustainable — community institutions, local civil
society and participatory governance. This program underpins everything else we do by strengthening local
ownership from the start.

## What this program delivers

- Community-driven development planning and participatory local governance
- Capacity strengthening for community organizations, civil society groups and local institutions
- Social accountability, citizen participation, inclusive dialogue and constructive engagement with duty bearers
- Support to local coordination, service mapping, referral networks and community-led monitoring
- Gender equality, disability inclusion, youth participation and leadership development
- Institutional capacity development, partnership strengthening, localization and evidence-informed advocacy
