LOCA strengthens household and community resilience in settings where climate shocks and conflict interact.
Our livelihoods programming connects immediate food security with the longer-term building blocks of durable,
inclusive local economies.

## What this program delivers

- Climate-smart agriculture, agroecology, kitchen/perma-gardens and sustainable natural resource management
- Food security, livelihood recovery, cash-based support and productive asset restoration
- Pastoral and agro-pastoral livelihood support, animal health linkages and market-oriented services
- Climate adaptation, disaster risk reduction, community-based natural resource governance and environmental restoration
- Village savings and loan associations (VSLA), savings groups, entrepreneurship and microenterprise development
- Youth and women's economic empowerment, employability, skills development and market linkages
